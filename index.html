Cadra Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Cadra ("we", "our", "us") operates the Cadra mobile application (the "App"). Cadra is operated by Ty Edmeades, operating as CADRA, in Ontario, Canada. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account and profile information

When you create an account or complete onboarding, we collect information you provide, such as your name, email address, cycling experience, goals, available training days and hours, and, if you choose to provide them, your functional threshold power (FTP) and heart rate details.

Workout and ride data

When you ride with the App, we record your workout data, including duration, heart rate, power, cadence, speed and distance (when your sensors or trainer provide them), your effort ratings, and ride timestamps. We also keep a second-by-second record of each ride so we can create a workout file (FIT file) for you.

Device and sensor data

With your permission, the App connects to Bluetooth sensors such as heart rate monitors, power meters, smart trainers and speed/cadence sensors during workouts.

Coach chat

If you use the in-app coach chat (Coach Marcus), we store your messages and the coach's replies so the conversation can continue and so we can keep the service safe and within usage limits.

App usage information

We record basic in-app events (for example, which onboarding screens were completed, or when a workout was started) to understand how the App is used and to improve it. This information is stored in our own database, linked to your account, and is not used for advertising or sold.

Payment information

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat, our subscription provider, processes purchase receipts to confirm your subscription status.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and adapt your personalized training plan
  • To provide coaching during workouts and through the coach chat
  • To record your rides and show your progress
  • To send your rides or workouts to accounts you choose to connect (see section 4)
  • To manage your account and subscription, and to send you account-related emails
  • To keep the App secure and to improve it

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. AI Transparency Statement

Cadra uses artificial intelligence provided by Anthropic (Claude) for two features:

  • Training plans and coaching notes. To build and adapt your plan, we send Anthropic your training profile (such as goal, experience, schedule and FTP) and a summary of your recent training (such as which workouts you completed, their duration and how hard they felt).
  • Coach chat. When you message the coach, your message, recent messages in the conversation, and relevant plan details are sent to Anthropic to generate a reply.

Anthropic processes this data to provide the service to us and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models. We do not use your data to train any AI model.

Data from connected accounts is never processed by AI. No data from Garmin, Strava or Wahoo, including account details, tokens or anything those services return, is ever sent to Anthropic or any other AI system, or used to train, evaluate or improve any AI model.

The coach chat is optional. You can choose not to use it at any time, and you can ask us to delete your chat history (see section 8).

4. Connected Accounts (Garmin, Strava, Wahoo)

You can choose to connect third-party accounts. Each connection is optional, starts only when you sign in to that service and give permission, and can be disconnected at any time in the App's settings. When you disconnect, we revoke our access with that service and delete the access tokens we stored. Cadra sends data to these accounts only at your direction; this is not a sale of your data.

Garmin Connect

If you connect Garmin, Cadra sends your upcoming planned workouts to your Garmin Connect account so they appear on your Garmin calendar and device. Cadra does not receive or store any Garmin activity, health or device data. We store only the access credentials Garmin issues for your account and a record of the workouts we created, so we can update or remove them when your plan changes. When you disconnect, we remove the workouts Cadra created. Workouts sent to Garmin are handled under Garmin's privacy notice.

Strava

If you connect Strava, Cadra uploads your completed Cadra rides to your Strava account (automatically, unless you turn this off). Cadra requests permission to upload activities only and does not read your Strava activities. We store your access credentials, your Strava display name, and the ID of each activity we upload so a ride is never uploaded twice. Uploaded activities follow your Strava privacy settings and Strava's privacy policy.

Wahoo

If you connect Wahoo, Cadra sends your upcoming planned workouts to your Wahoo account so they appear in the Wahoo app and on Wahoo devices. Cadra does not read your Wahoo workout history. We store your access credentials, your Wahoo display name, and a record of the workouts we created so we can update or remove them. Workouts sent to Wahoo are handled under Wahoo's privacy policy.

5. Service Providers

We share data only with these providers, who help us run the App:

  • Supabase: database, file storage, authentication and server functions (hosts all App data, including connected-account credentials)
  • Anthropic: AI plan generation, coaching notes and coach chat (see section 3)
  • ElevenLabs: generates the coach's spoken voice during workouts from coaching text
  • RevenueCat: subscription management
  • Apple: app distribution, payments, and app install attribution for our App Store ads
  • Resend: sends account emails, such as email confirmation
  • Garmin, Strava and Wahoo: only if you connect them (see section 4)

None of these providers receives data from your Garmin, Strava or Wahoo accounts, except the service you connected receiving the workouts or rides you chose to send it.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored with Supabase, encrypted in transit and at rest. Access controls ensure each user can access only their own data, and connected-account credentials can be read only by our servers, never by the App. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Connected-account credentials are deleted when you disconnect that account. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep it by law.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You can at any time:

  • Access or export your data: ask us for a copy
  • Correct your data: edit your profile in the App or contact us
  • Delete your data: delete your account in the App or contact us
  • Withdraw consent: disconnect any connected account in the App's settings, stop using the coach chat, or contact us

Residents of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions may have additional rights, including data portability and the right to object to processing. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

9. Children's Privacy

Cadra is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and if we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes in the App or by other means. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
